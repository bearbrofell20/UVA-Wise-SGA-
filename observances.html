Holidays & Observances

Students at the University of Virginia's College at Wise come from many backgrounds, faiths, and cultures. This calendar lists the national holidays and the religious and cultural observances of the 2026–2027 academic year so that every member of our community is recognized.

A note on dates. Many religious observances follow a lunar or other calendar. They often begin the evening before the date listed, may span several days, and can fall a day earlier or later depending on local observation and tradition.

This calendar covers the 2026–2027 academic year and is reviewed annually. If an observance is missing or listed incorrectly, please tell us and we will correct it.
