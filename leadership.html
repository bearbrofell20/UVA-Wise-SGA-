Your Elected Leaders

Meet the students representing you this year. Every one of them was elected by their peers — and every one of them is here to serve.

Executive Board

The officers leading SGA

The Executive Board serves as the chair of the Senate, represents the student body before the administration, and oversees the day-to-day operations of SGA. It consists of the President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, and the Student Member of the Board.
