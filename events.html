Weekly Meetings

Every student is welcome

SGA meets every week during the fall and spring semesters. Any student can come, watch, and speak to the Senate during the open floor.

  • Bring a concern, idea, or proposal to the open floor
  • See how the activity fee is being spent
  • Find out what's being voted on that week
  • No sign-up needed. Just show up.

Meeting Snapshot

When: Fridays at 1 p.m.
Where: Dogwood Room, 5th Floor, Slemp Student Center
Who: All UVA Wise students. Open to the public.
Agenda: Posted ahead of each meeting
