About the SGA

SGA is elected by students and answers to students. Here's how it's set up and what it does.

Our Mission

What we're here for

SGA speaks for the students at UVA Wise on anything that affects student life or the college. If you're a degree-seeking student here, you're already a member.

We're the link between students and the people who run the college. We bring student concerns forward, support clubs and events, and work on making campus better. Our motto sums it up: Cavs Help Cavs.

Of the students,
by the students,
for the students.
How We Work

How we work

Open

Our meetings are open and our decisions are public. Any student can see what we're working on.

For everyone

We represent every student here. Anyone can come to a meeting or bring something up.

Practical

We take what students tell us and try to actually fix it.

How We're Organized

How SGA is set up

SGA has three parts.

Executive Board

Five elected officers who run SGA and lead the meetings: the President, Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary, and Student Member of the Board.

The Senate

Senators who speak for each class, plus at-large and international seats. They vote on what SGA does and recognize new clubs.

Committees

Eight committees that each handle a specific area, from advocacy to campus life, and bring their work back to the Senate.

Our History

Student leadership since 1954

UVA Wise opened in 1954, and students have had a hand in campus life ever since. A lot of the traditions and clubs here started because students pushed for them.

That keeps going with every new group of students, including you if you want to be part of it.

Meet this year's members

  • Elections held every year
  • Open meetings each fall and spring semester
  • A say in how the student activity budget is spent
  • Works with clubs, athletics, and campus life

Want to be part of it?

Come to a meeting, run for a seat, or start a club. Anyone can get involved.
