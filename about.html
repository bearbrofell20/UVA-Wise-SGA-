About the SGA

Elected by students, accountable to students. Here's who we are and how we work on your behalf.

Our Mission

Representation, service, and student advocacy

Established under the charter of the University of Virginia's College at Wise, the Student Government Association exists to articulate and implement the views of the Student Body and to further the general welfare of both the students and the College. Every degree-seeking student is a member of SGA.

We serve as the vital link between students and the administration, faculty, and staff — carrying student concerns forward, supporting organizations and activities, and working to improve the College community. In everything we do, one promise guides us: Cavs Help Cavs.

Of the students,
by the students,
for the students.
What Guides Us

Our core values

Integrity

We hold ourselves to the highest standard of transparency and act in the genuine best interest of the students we serve.

Inclusion

Every student deserves a seat at the table. We work to make sure all voices — across every background — are heard.

Action

Ideas matter, but results matter more. We turn student feedback into tangible improvements on campus.

How We're Organized

The structure of your government

SGA is organized into three working parts that keep our government balanced, accountable, and effective.

Executive Board

The President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Student Member of the Board lead the organization, chair the Senate, and represent students to the administration and beyond.

The Senate

Class senators along with international and at-large representatives express the concerns of the Student Body, charter and support organizations, and vote on the issues that affect campus life.

Committees

Eight standing committees — from Advocacy & Opportunity to Campus Life — dig into the details and bring recommendations to the full Senate.

Our History

A tradition of student leadership

Since the college was founded in 1954, students have shaped the direction of campus life at Wise. Generations of student leaders have advocated for their peers, launched beloved traditions, and built the vibrant community we enjoy today.

That legacy continues with every elected class — and with you, if you choose to get involved.

  • Weekly public sessions in the fall and spring semesters
  • Oversight of the student activity budget on your behalf
  • Partnerships with clubs, athletics, and campus life
