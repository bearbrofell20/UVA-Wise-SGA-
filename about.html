About the SGA

The Student Government Association is elected by students and accountable to students. This page outlines how it is organized and what it does.

Our Mission

Our Purpose

The Student Government Association represents the students of the University of Virginia's College at Wise in all matters affecting student life and the College. Every degree-seeking student is a member.

We serve as the link between the student body and the College's administration, faculty, and staff. We carry student concerns forward, support organizations and activities, and work to improve the campus community. Our motto captures it: Cavs Help Cavs.

Of the students,
by the students,
for the students.
Our Approach

How We Work

Open

Our meetings and decisions are open to the student body. Any student may see what the Association is working on.

For Everyone

We represent every student at the College, and any student may attend a meeting or raise a concern.

Practical

We turn student feedback into concrete improvements on campus.

Structure

How SGA Is Organized

The Association is organized into three parts.

Executive Board

The President, Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary are elected by the student body. The Student Member of the Board is appointed. Together, the five officers lead SGA and chair its meetings.

The Senate

Senators represent each class, along with at-large and international seats. They vote on the Association's business and charter new student organizations.

Committees

Eight committees each oversee a specific area, from advocacy to campus life, and report their work to the Senate.

Our History

Student Leadership Since 1954

The University of Virginia's College at Wise opened in 1954, and students have helped shape campus life ever since. Many of the College's traditions and organizations exist because students advocated for them.

That work continues with every new class of students, including you, should you choose to take part.

Meet this year's members

  • Elections held every year
  • Open meetings each fall and spring semester
  • A say in how the student activity budget is spent
  • Works with clubs, athletics, and campus life
Everyone Belongs Here

Inclusion

The Association represents every student at UVA Wise, without exception. We do not discriminate on the basis of age, color, disability, gender identity or expression, national or ethnic origin, political affiliation, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, veteran status, or any other part of who you are.

We also maintain a calendar of national holidays and religious and cultural observances so that no one is overlooked. If you ever feel excluded or unheard, please reach out. That is precisely what we are here for.

Want to be part of it?

Attend a meeting, run for a seat, or start a club. Every student is welcome to take part.
