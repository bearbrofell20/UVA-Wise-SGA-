What We Do

Working for you, every day

SGA is the official governing body of the student population. We give students a formal voice in college decisions and put resources directly into the hands of our community.

Advocacy

We carry student concerns to faculty, administration, and the Board of Visitors — from dining and housing to academic policy and campus safety.

Funding

Through the Student Activity Fee, SGA charters and funds student organizations, events, and initiatives that make campus life vibrant.

Community

We host traditions, town halls, and service events that bring the Cavalier family together and strengthen the ties between students.

Who We Are

A student-led voice with real influence

The Student Government Association is made up of your elected peers — students who chose to step up and serve. We meet regularly in open sessions, debate the issues that matter, and turn student ideas into action.

  • Elected executive board and Senate representing every class
  • Open weekly meetings any student can attend
  • Direct line to college administration and staff
  • Oversight of the student activity budget
