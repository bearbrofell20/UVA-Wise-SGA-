Get Involved

There are a few ways to get involved with SGA, whether you want to run for a seat or just come to a meeting.

Four Ways In

Ways to get involved

Run for Office

Campaign for an executive or Senate seat and represent your class directly.

Attend a Meeting

SGA meetings are open to everyone. Come by Fridays at 1 p.m. to bring up an idea or just see how it works.

Charter a Club

Start a new club and make it official through SGA.

Request Funding

Apply for financial support for your organization's events and projects.

Committees

Our committees

Each committee handles one area and works with a campus office. Every senator sits on a committee, so this is where a lot of the work happens once you're elected.

Advocacy & Opportunity

Promotes belonging and welcome for all students, planning events around diversity, equity, and inclusion.

Student Engagement

Works with the Office of Student Engagement to keep club and organization chartering efficient.

Campus Life

Looks after buildings, dorms, grounds, and dining, working with Chartwells, Residence Life, and facilities.

Academic & Student Affairs

Works with the academic dean and departments and keeps students informed of academic changes.

Events

Plans and executes campus traditions across the fall and spring, including Homecoming and Cavapalooza.

Leadership & Local Outreach

Connects SGA with campus and community leaders and serves the town of Wise through outreach.

Election & Policy

Amends and upholds the bylaws of the Student Government Association on behalf of the student body.

Enrollment

Works with Admissions and senior leadership to support the College's growth and the student experience.

Elections

Run for a seat

Students elect the executive board and Senate every year. It's more straightforward than most people expect. Here's what it takes:

  • Be a student in good academic standing
  • Pick up a candidacy packet from the SGA office
  • Collect the required signatures from other students
  • Campaign, then let students vote

Ask About Candidacy

Election Timeline

Fall: Freshman & special elections open in September.

Spring: General elections for the executive board and all Senate seats.

Anytime: Open seats can be filled by appointment. Reach out if you're interested mid-year.

Steps to Charter

  1. Gather at least the minimum number of founding members.
  2. Draft a simple constitution and mission statement.
  3. Find a faculty or staff advisor.
  4. Submit your charter application to SGA.
  5. Present to the Senate for approval.
Student Organizations

Charter a new club

Want to start a club that doesn't exist yet? SGA is how student organizations become official. Once you're chartered, you can request funding, reserve campus space, and put on events. The Student Engagement Committee and the Office of Student Engagement handle the process.

Clubs at Wise range from academic groups to service organizations to intramural teams.

Start a Charter

Allocation Requests

Request funding for your org

SGA allocates student activity funds to support events, travel, supplies, and programming that benefit the student body. If your chartered organization has a project in mind, submit an Allocation Request and we'll take it from there.

  • Open to all chartered student organizations
  • Submit an Allocation Request before the posted deadline
  • Present your request to SGA for review
  • Receive a funding decision from the Senate

Submit a Request

Good to Know

Requests are reviewed on a rolling basis while funds remain. Submitting early gives your organization the best chance of approval.

Questions about eligibility or the process? The Treasurer and Finance Committee are happy to help you prepare.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to be elected to be involved in SGA?

You don't need a seat to take part. Any student can attend meetings, volunteer at events, charter or join a club, and bring ideas to the open floor. Committee work, though, is for elected members: senators and officers staff the committees.

How much time does it take?

It depends on your role. Coming to a meeting is about an hour a week. Serving as a senator or officer, plus the committee work that comes with it, takes more. The easiest way to start is to show up to a Friday meeting.

Who can charter a club?

Any group of currently enrolled students with a shared interest, a willing advisor, and a simple constitution can apply to charter a new organization through SGA.

When are elections held?

Freshman and special elections are held in the fall, and general elections for all seats take place in the spring. Watch the events page and your student email for exact dates.

How do I bring an issue to SGA?

Come to any meeting and speak during the open floor, talk to a senator directly, or use our contact form.

Not sure where to start?

Just reach out. Tell us what you're interested in and we'll point you in the right direction.
